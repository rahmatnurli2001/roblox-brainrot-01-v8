Brainrot Carnival is a bright 3D Steal-a-Brainrot style carnival game built for the make.fun roblox-brainrot-01 competition ($1,000 prize pool, top 50 entries paid): buy wacky Brainrot characters as they parade past, fill your carnival booth with them to earn money every second, steal Brainrots from rival booths while defending your own, and rebirth for a permanent income multiplier.

## How to play

- Walk up to the Parade and press E to buy the Brainrot on show.
- Carry it to your booth and deposit it — it earns you money every second.
- Sneak into a rival's booth to steal their Brainrots, then run back to yours.
- Lock your booth and press Q to shield it while rivals are nearby.
- Rebirth once your booth is full to earn a permanent income multiplier.
